LOAST Ltd Privacy Notice

Version 1.0 · Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Who we are

LOAST Ltd ("LOAST", "we", "us") is a London-based organisation. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this notice. This notice explains how we handle personal data about our employees, contractors, and external guests (such as partners and suppliers) who use our Microsoft cloud services.

2. How to contact us

Our privacy contact handles all questions about personal data:

  • Email: privacy@loastlab.example
  • Post: Privacy Team, LOAST Ltd, London, United Kingdom

3. What personal data we collect

Type of dataExamples
Identity and contact dataName, job title, department, work email address, phone number
Account dataUsername, group memberships, assigned roles and licences
Authentication dataSign-in methods registered for multi-factor authentication (for example, an authenticator app or phone number)
Sign-in and activity logsDate and time of sign-in, IP address, approximate location, device and browser details, applications accessed
Device dataDevice name, operating system, compliance status of company-managed devices
Guest dataFor external guests: name, email address, home organisation, and the resources you are invited to

4. Why we use your data and our lawful basis

PurposeLawful basis (UK GDPR)
Creating and managing your account so you can access our systemsContract (for employees and contractors) or legitimate interests (for guests)
Protecting accounts and data, including multi-factor authentication, Conditional Access, and risk detectionLegitimate interests in keeping our systems secure
Keeping audit and sign-in logs to investigate security incidentsLegitimate interests and legal obligations
Reviewing who has access to what (access reviews)Legitimate interests in limiting access to those who need it
Managing company devicesContract and legitimate interests

5. Who we share your data with

  • Microsoft, as our cloud service provider (data processor) for Microsoft Entra ID and related services.
  • IT support providers who help run our systems, under a written contract.
  • Auditors and regulators, where required by law or for compliance audits.

We do not sell personal data.

6. International transfers

Some of our service providers may process data outside the UK. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Addendum to standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep your data

DataRetention period
Employee and contractor accountsDisabled when you leave and deleted after 30 days
Guest accountsRemoved when no longer needed, checked by regular access reviews
Sign-in and audit logsUp to 1 year for security investigations

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data in certain circumstances;
  • ask us to restrict or object to how we use your data;
  • ask for your data in a portable format.

To use any of these rights, email our privacy contact. We will respond within one month.

9. How we protect your data

We protect accounts with multi-factor authentication, Conditional Access policies, least-privilege administrator roles, and regular access reviews. Access to personal data is limited to people who need it for their job.

10. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

11. Changes to this notice

We review this notice at least once a year. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.

LOAST Ltd is fictional. This sample notice was written for an SC-300 identity lab and portfolio. It is not legal advice.
